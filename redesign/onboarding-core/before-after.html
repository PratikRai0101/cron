BEFORE / AFTER

The same app,
five screens that did nothing.

The old first run asked for nothing, showed nothing and taught nothing. The new one asks two questions and answers them with the app's own rock.

  • CUTThe splash screen. It announced the app instead of starting it.
  • CUT"Widgets are coming later." A screen that exists to admit a feature does not exist.
  • CUTThe four-dot stepper. Three screens now, and a back chevron to move between them.
  • CUTTwo different "Skip" buttons, and a skip that seeded an identity called "Focus".
  • KEPTThe four areas and their descriptions, the 60-minute default, the 15-minute steps, one identity on the free plan.
  • MADEThe core column: four identities as four layers of rock, the chosen one dyed.
  • MADEThe goal as a depth. One day of work is a band cut to your goal, and the band grows as you drag.
  • MADEA closing screen that shows your core with today's slot waiting, and one button: start.
