How it moves

The day's band follows the goal. A critically damped spring, 230 stiffness / 25 damping, no bounce, interruptible mid-drag.

Haptics

Selection tick when the ruler snaps to a step. One light impact when the band settles. Nothing on every frame.

Reduced motion

The band re-cuts in place over 120 ms with no spring and no impact. The number updates immediately.

The same motion, later

Finishing a session drops a band onto the core in the app. The onboarding teaches that spring first.
